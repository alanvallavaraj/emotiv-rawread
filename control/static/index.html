1 · Source

Discovery covers both transports: it scans Bluetooth Low Energy for about eight seconds, then lists any Emotiv USB receiver/dongle attached. The transport of each result is shown on its row.

3 · Electrode signal spread

Measured here from the samples themselves — the standard deviation of each electrode over the last few seconds — not the headset's own contact-quality field, which this path does not decode. A well-seated electrode on a resting head reads roughly 10–50 µV. Much higher means a loose electrode, movement or muscle activity; near zero means no signal at all.
